✳ DRAWDUEL — Art battles with friends. Local mode works offline; online mode requires the game server.

🎨 How to play

1. Set up a room or local match, then each player enters ONE new prompt before each round. The game chooses one for both artists to draw.
2. Hit Start drawing. Both players draw the same challenge on separate canvases until time runs out.
3. Compare the artwork and vote. Most points after the final round wins!

Your drawing toolkit: Pencil, marker, spray, eraser, paint bucket, line, rectangle, ellipse, color picker and text. Each player gets their own colors, sizes, undo/redo, grid, filled-shape toggle and PNG download.

Shortcuts: Click a canvas to make it active, then press B (brush), M (marker), S (spray), E (eraser), G (fill), L (line), R (rectangle), O (ellipse), I (eyedropper), T (text). Ctrl/Cmd + Z to undo, Ctrl/Cmd + Y or Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z to redo. [ and ] change size.

Choose Start Local Battle for two artists on one device, or Create Online Room to invite a friend on another device. The room host controls the clock and next rounds. Both online players vote; agreement picks a winner, otherwise it is a tie. Online rooms are temporary, so save art as PNG before leaving.
